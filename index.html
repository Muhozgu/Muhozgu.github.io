About

Between the claim and the dashboard

I work where insurance operations meet data. At Marsh, that means opening and triaging a high-volume book of motor claims, running fault analysis, and reporting decisions back to insurers — work that runs on Jira and on getting the details right at volume.

Alongside that, I've spent the past two years building the technical side of the same problem: evaluating AI systems, automating operational workflows, and building the pipelines, dashboards, and reports that operations teams actually read. I moved to Poland at 17 to study Computer Science, specializing in AI and Data Science, and the two halves — operations and engineering — keep ending up in the same projects.
